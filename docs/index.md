# DietFlow

**Meal Planner Widget: DietFlow** puts your meal plan on your iPhone's Home Screen and Lock
Screen. Plan once; the widget shows what to eat now and next, and moves on by itself.

- [Support](support)
- [Privacy Policy](privacy)
- [Terms of Use](terms)

The assistant in DietFlow uses OpenAI's GPT models. Text is sent to OpenAI only after you agree in
the app; the [Privacy Policy](privacy) has the details.
