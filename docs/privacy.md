# DietFlow Privacy Policy

*Last updated 6 October 2026.*

DietFlow is a meal plan app for iPhone. This page says what happens to what you put into it.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your meal plans, what you mark as done or skipped, your reminders and your settings are stored on
your iPhone, in the app and its widget. They are not sent to us. DietFlow has no account and no
advertising.

Reading a plan from pasted text, a file, a photo or a PDF happens on your iPhone.

## The assistant

If you use the assistant — to put a list in order, or to have a plan written — the text you give
it is sent to our server and passed to an AI service that reads it and writes the plan. This is
the only time anything you type or paste leaves your iPhone.

- The text is used to produce your plan and for nothing else.
- We do not store it and do not log it. Our server keeps only technical records: status codes,
  sizes and counts.
- To limit misuse, the request carries a random identifier created when the app was installed. It
  is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your device's identifiers.
- The AI service receives the text in order to answer, and handles it under its own terms.

Do not put anything in a request that you would not want processed this way. The assistant writes
everyday meal plans; it does not give medical advice.

## Anonymous usage data

To help improve the app, DietFlow shares anonymous usage data with PostHog, an analytics service.
It is on by default; turn it off at any time in *Settings › Share Anonymous Usage Data*, and
nothing more is sent.

What is shared:

- which features are used (a plan was imported, the assistant was used, a reminder setting changed),
  with counts such as how many days and meals a plan has;
- where a limit was reached, and whether a purchase was started, completed or cancelled;
- that the app was opened, installed or updated, its version and its language.

What is never shared: the names of your meals or plans, your notes, anything you paste or type,
photos, or files.

The data is tied to a random number created on your iPhone. It is not linked to your name, your
Apple Account or your device's own identifiers, and it is not used for advertising or to follow
you across other apps or websites.

## Purchases

DietFlow Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we do not receive your
name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app asks the App Store on your iPhone
whether a purchase is active.

## Your choices

Deleting a plan removes it from your iPhone. *Settings › Delete All Data* removes everything.
Deleting the app removes its data, apart from a small counter of how much of the assistant's
allowance was used, which is kept in your iPhone's Keychain so that reinstalling does not reset it.
