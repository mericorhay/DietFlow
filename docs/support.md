# DietFlow Support

Help with DietFlow ("Meal Planner Widget: DietFlow") for iPhone.

## Get in touch

Write to <mericorhayy@gmail.com>. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which iPhone
and iOS version you use. We answer in English and Turkish.

Bugs can also be reported at <https://github.com/mericorhay/DietFlow/issues>. Issues there are
public, so leave out anything private.

## Common questions

**How do I add the widget?**
Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap *Edit*, then *Add Widget*, search for
DietFlow and pick a widget and a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap
*Customize* and add it there. There are three widgets: Next Meal, Today's Meals and Day Progress.

**The widget shows the wrong meal, or says to open the app.**
Open DietFlow once: it rewrites what the widget shows. iOS decides when widgets are redrawn, and
can delay them in Low Power Mode.

**How do I change the widget's colour?**
In the app, open the *Widgets* tab and choose a colour and a background. With tinted or clear
icons on your Home Screen, and on the Lock Screen, iOS chooses the colours itself.

**How long does a meal stay on the widget?**
One hour after its time, then the next meal takes its place. Change it under *Widgets › Keep a
Meal in Front For*.

**How do I bring in a plan I already have?**
*Import Plan*: paste the text, choose a file, or scan a photo or a PDF. The plan is shown for
review before it is saved. The assistant can put an untidy list in order first.

**Which AI does the assistant use?**
OpenAI's GPT models. The text you give the assistant is sent to OpenAI through our server, after
you agree in the app. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**The assistant says I have no requests left.**
The allowance comes back on the date shown under the assistant in *Import Plan*. DietFlow Plus
has a larger one.

**How do I cancel DietFlow Plus?**
*Settings › your name › Subscriptions* on your iPhone, or *Settings › DietFlow Plus › Manage
Subscription* in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**I bought Plus but the app does not show it.**
In the app: *Settings › DietFlow Plus › Restore Purchases*, signed in to the same Apple Account.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.

**How do I delete my data?**
*Settings › Delete All Data* in the app. Everything is on your iPhone; there is no account to
close.
